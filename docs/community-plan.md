# A practical first 30 days

- **Days 1–7:** Review launch checks and policies. Complete one first-time contribution walkthrough. Prioritize accessibility and device feedback. Keep the board current and respond kindly to questions.
- **Days 8–14:** Ask the founder to authorize any external outreach. Identify one potential food-relief organization through official sources; invite feedback without implying a partnership. Recruit a fluent reviewer before committing to a translation release.
- **Days 15–21:** If a willing organization submits a request, review consent and safeguards. Help a few newcomers complete useful tasks and publish only accepted, evidence-linked contributions. Improve instructions from their feedback.
- **Days 22–30:** Publish an evidence-based progress note, even if no organization request is ready. Archive stale tasks, review maintenance effort, and choose the next small milestone. Consider a For Good First Issue listing only after its criteria are met.

Measure accepted useful work, review responsiveness, and verified organization needs—not stars. No invitations or outreach messages are sent automatically by this plan. Funding applications, accounts, and commitments require explicit founder approval.
