# Prospective pilot: Dearborn Community Food Pantry

**Status on 2026-11-18: PUBLIC-SOURCE RESEARCH ONLY. No contact, consent, partnership, approved volunteer shifts or verified field outcomes.**

This folder documents a prospective OneMealForward pilot, not a partner listing or endorsement. Volunteers may propose project deliverables here, but they must not represent themselves as volunteers authorized by the pantry or as agents of OneMealForward. The pantry can decline without explanation. No pantry logo or private correspondence may be published.

## Why this candidate

The Dearborn Community Food Pantry is a real monthly grocery-distribution collaboration of Good Shepherd UMC and Dearborn First UMC. Unlike a hypothetical charity, it has a published third-Saturday distribution schedule and a public volunteer coordination channel.

Official sources (checked 2026-11-18):
- Dearborn First UMC, **Food Pantry Ministries**: https://dearbornfirstumc.squarespace.com/groups/food-pantry
- City of Dearborn, **Local Organizations Providing Food Assistance**: https://dearborn.gov/local-organizations-providing-food-assistance

The source pages describe a food distribution on the **third Saturday each month, 9–10 a.m., Good Shepherd UMC, 1570 Mason St., Dearborn** and volunteers arriving **8:15–8:30 a.m.** The church site lists an event for **Saturday, November 21, 2026**. These are published schedules **not** confirmation of an available volunteer place. Independently confirm current availability using the original organization's process before attending.

The church lists **missions@dearbornfirstumc.org** for food pantry volunteer information and coordination. This is a public, role-based contact provided for inquiries, **not** evidence that OneMealForward has reached the organization.

## Four tightly scoped project tasks

1. **#36 — Consent and choice of deliverable (founder/maintainer gate).** Seek founder approval before outreach; ask whether one small, no-cost deliverable would genuinely help. Get scoped permission to publish a listing or name the organization as a collaborator; the pantry chooses and approves the task.
2. **#37 — Research public volunteer information.** Independently verify the official schedule/contact and produce a sourced, dated review. May proceed without contacting the pantry or claiming partnership.
3. **#38 — Draft an accessible volunteer handout.** Unapproved proposal only. If the pantry requests one, adapt it to verified official instructions and publish or distribute it only with staff permission.
4. **#39 — Design a privacy-safe outcome checklist.** Propose easy, opt-in staff verification of deliverable use and other permitted aggregate evidence, without names or aid-recipient data.

These tasks are legitimate project research/design issues, **not verified organization volunteer shifts**. Don't add them as organization records to the public mission board until consent and review criteria in docs/verification.md are met.

## First contact and approval

The founder may send the draft in outreach-draft.md through the public coordinator email if they choose. Do not send it automatically or imply that the pantry has already accepted. A positive response alone does not authorize public listings, logos, data sharing or posting third-party content. Ask explicitly what can be published and whether the pantry can provide a public confirmation reference.

If there is no response, do not follow up repeatedly or list the pantry as a OneMealForward partner. Continue independent documentation research or select another candidate. If approval is withdrawn, suspend the proposed partnership and any associated publication.

## Evidence ladder and reporting

| Stage | What it means | Allowed public record |
|---|---|---|
| Research | Official published schedule verified | Source URLs and check date; **not an impact claim** |
| Draft | Volunteer produces a proposed deliverable | Reviewed project PR; no claim of pantry adoption |
| Accepted | Authorized coordinator explicitly accepts the deliverable | Only the public-safe acknowledgment they permitted; not yet actual use |
| Used | Coordinator confirms handout/page/service was actually used | Dated, consented evidence of usage; avoid personal data |
| Outcome | Organization verifies an appropriate aggregate result using an agreed method | Describe measure, timeframe, source, limitations; never invent meals or people helped |

Never collect aid-recipient identities, personal food-assistance situations, volunteer names/contact information, private emails, health data, photographs or internal operations records. Do not create an independent volunteer signup system, reroute volunteers, or promise shifts. Follow the pantry's rules and its own registration process.

The project impact ledger measures **reviewed contributions**, not food distributed. Field outcomes require separate explicit evidence and permission to publish, consistent with docs/impact.md.

## Release gate

Before adding a verified organization record to the website, require:
- Independently corroborated official organization identity and request.
- Direct permission from an authorized coordinator for the specific work, name use, public listing and safe evidence.
- Verified official volunteer registration/accessibility/safeguarding instructions.
- A reviewer, verification date, and expiration within 90 days.
- No private correspondence or personal data in GitHub.
- A reviewed PR passing the existing data validation, unit and browser checks.

The prospective pilot does not alter site/data/missions.json or site/data/impact.json. The public site continues to say no verified food-relief partners until a genuine agreement is documented.
