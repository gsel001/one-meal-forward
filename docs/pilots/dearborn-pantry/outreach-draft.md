I'm starting OneMealForward, a small, free open-source volunteer initiative based around practical work against food insecurity.

If you are interested, could you tell me:
- Which one task, if any, would help the pantry most?
- Who could confirm the content and authorize any public use?

If this isn't the right fit, no problem at all. Thank you for the work your pantry does for Dearborn neighbors.

Best regards,
