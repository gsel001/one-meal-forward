## Why this position exists

OneMealForward should recognize the first person other than its founder to join its community, without turning a volunteer role into a permanent privilege over other people. The designation **Founding Maintainer** is a project governance title, separate from GitHub's built-in **Maintain** repository permission and separate from historical founder ownership.

Following the nominee's affirmative acceptance, record two different things:

1. **Founding recognition:** `First Community Founding Maintainer — @Elizabethjulie`, recognizing early service. This historical distinction does not expire automatically. It can be corrected if objectively inaccurate, but never erased as punishment for respectful dissent.
2. **Operational appointment:** a maintainership with defined responsibilities, limited GitHub access, a reviewed 12-month term and a renewal or succession process. Technical permission can be suspended during a real incident, independently reviewed, or removed for cause.

## Extra protection for this position

1. **Nonretaliation:** A founder, council or administrator cannot legitimately demote, threaten, exclude or revoke someone's operational role solely for a good-faith security report, reasonable disagreement, refusal to approve an unsafe PR, or participation in an appeal.
2. **Separation of review:** No person who is accused in a dispute (including the founder) may serve as the sole investigator, decision-maker or appeal judge concerning the Founding Maintainer.
3. **Proportionate due process:** For nonemergency involuntary removal, give written, evidenced reasons, a response period of at least seven days, and a recommendation from at least one uninvolved qualified engineering reviewer and one nonconflicted governance/safety reviewer. An elected council, once active, must approve removal by at least two-thirds of its **nonconflicted non-subject members** at its normal quorum. With too few independent reviewers, defer permanent removal or seek neutral external review; do not fabricate signoffs.
4. **Emergency limitation:** An account compromise, credible threat, or active privacy/security incident may justify immediate **temporary limitation of technical access**, no broader than necessary. Document the reason privately, publish only a safe summary, and request independent review within 72 hours when a qualified person is available. Emergency restrictions do not erase founding recognition or establish permanent guilt.
5. **Appeal:** The affected person can seek review from a different impartial human or panel. If no independent appeal exists yet, state this limitation and do not call a unilateral decision an independent adjudication.
6. **No exception to code safeguards:** The Founding Maintainer also cannot self-approve PRs, override CI, bypass a safety hold, change branch rules unilaterally or grant themselves extra privileges. Everyone, including the founder, follows the same independent gates.
7. **Recognition changes are protected:** The factual first-member designation may only be corrected with published objective evidence, prior notice and independent review. It may not be deleted through routine politics or a unilateral documentation edit. After the Charter is adopted, attempts to weaken these protections follow its constitutional amendment procedure.
