# Community Rights, Concerns, and Independent Appeals

> **Proposed policy, not ratified.** The existing [Code of Conduct](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), [Security Policy](../../SECURITY.md), and GitHub reporting tools still apply. This document does not claim that an independent panel or confidential reporting service already exists.

## Rights of every participant

Whether founder, council representative, contributor, first-time volunteer, technical reviewer, or visitor, you may:

- Respectfully question the founder, governance policy, technical implementation, or another leader without losing access **because of disagreement alone**.
- Propose improvements, request a decision explanation, report unsafe behavior, and appeal a disciplinary outcome.
- Receive consistent, evidence-based treatment under the Code of Conduct.
- Decline a task, disclose a conflict, or step away without coercion.
- Receive notice and a chance to respond before serious permanent discipline, except for proportionate urgent security containment.
- Expect reports involving safety, harassment, or sensitive information to be handled with limited access and an appropriate public summary rather than unnecessary disclosure.

No one receives GitHub write or administrator access merely because they speak, vote, contribute, or have a title.

## Where to raise concerns

| Concern | Appropriate channel | Response and safeguards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Idea, ordinary design disagreement, priority | Public GitHub Discussion or governance proposal | Acknowledge, seek feedback, explain decision; retaliation prohibited |
| Incorrect PR, accessibility issue, unsafe code | PR review or issue, excluding exploit details | Independent code owner and, if needed, security reviewer; do not bypass CI |
| Vulnerability or exploit details | Private route in [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md) | Do not post exploitation details publicly; coordinate narrow mitigation |
| Harassment, privacy violation, conflict or abuse of power | Nonconflicted conduct/safety contacts once appointed; until then GitHub reporting channels or published project contact | Minimize disclosure; do not promise independent internal handling before staff exist |
| Concern involving founder | Independent safety/ethics stewards when appointed; neutral third-party review if needed | Founder must recuse from adjudication, may respond, cannot veto an investigation |
| Dispute over safety hold | Request written review by a different qualified technical reviewer **and** a nonconflicted safety steward | No unreviewed deployment while dispute is unresolved |

## A fair process in six steps

1. **Receive and protect:** acknowledge the concern without demanding unnecessary personal data. Reduce access to sensitive information.
2. **Separate conflicts:** anyone accused, involved or materially benefiting from the result recuses from adjudicating. There must be a genuinely independent reviewer.
3. **Explain the case:** when safe, tell the affected person the alleged behavior and key evidence, and allow a response.
4. **Investigate proportionately:** consider corroboration, both sides, risk, context and privacy. Respect good-faith criticism.
5. **Reach and record a reasoned outcome:** no action, coaching, mediation where safe, warning, task restriction, temporary suspension, or for-cause removal. A public record summarizes the process without exposing private data.
6. **Allow appeal:** a different nonconflicted panel examines substantial procedural or evidentiary errors. Permanent founder removal follows the more demanding Charter Article 9 process.

**Service target, not guaranteed SLA:** acknowledge within seven calendar days when capacity allows. Active threats may require immediate protective action; review the intervention within 72 hours when independent personnel are available.

## What prevents misuse of these protections?

- Criticism of a leader or a maintainer **is not misconduct by itself**.
- False accusations knowingly made in bad faith can be addressed through the same fair-process rules, not automatic punishment.
- An independent safety hold must specify a concrete risk, affected changes, needed evidence, and an appeal route. Reviewers cannot indefinitely block releases for unrelated personal disputes.
- The founder cannot remove a reviewer for refusing to approve an unsafe PR, replace the reviewer mid-dispute solely to secure approval, or change rules to bypass a safety hold.
- The council cannot depose the founder because of political opposition. Serious founder for-cause proceedings require objective findings and Article 9's independent protections.
- Reviewers and stewards are also subject to complaints, recusal and accountability.

## Current limitations

Until at least two qualified independent advisers and a functioning panel exist, **there is no independent internal appeals process**. State this clearly when a complaint cannot be resolved fairly within the project. Use GitHub Trust & Safety for platform abuse, and seek external neutral assistance where appropriate. Do not claim confidential, legal, or professional investigations are available without actual arrangements.

Humanitarian safeguarding also includes food-relief organizations and beneficiaries. External partners control authorization of their own work, and individual food recipients must not be treated as project publicity or impact statistics without informed consent.
