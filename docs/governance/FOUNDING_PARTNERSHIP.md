# Founding partnership and shared governance

**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-11-21  
**Status:** Discussion draft; not adopted.

This is an invitation to develop a lightweight founding partnership together. It proposes equal project leadership for two consenting founding partners; it does not announce an appointment, establish legal or financial ownership, grant GitHub permissions, or supersede [current governance](../../GOVERNANCE.md). The broader proposal in [PR #51](https://github.com/gsel001/one-meal-forward/pull/51) remains a reference for discussion.

## 1. Our purpose

OneMealForward is an independent, open-source volunteer initiative to help fight hunger through practical, authorized technical assistance. The mission comes before personal recognition, financial interests or individual authority. We value integrity, accessibility, compassion and verifiable outcomes.

## 2. Founding partnership

Proposed: the Founder and Founding Maintainer, if both explicitly agree, would have equal standing and equal (50/50) authority over reserved project decisions. Their distinct titles recognize their historical contributions, not a hierarchy. Historical recognition does not confer immunity from accountability. Neither partner is subordinate to the other.

Equal leadership is a proposal, not a division of equity, intellectual property, revenue or legal title. Any such arrangements must be addressed separately.

## 3. Decisions and responsibilities

Both partners would have an equal voice. Neither would hold a casting vote. Major decisions require explicit agreement from both, recorded in an appropriate decision log:

- Mission, governance, strategy and material external commitments.
- Ownership, additional co-owners, administrative access, security policy and repository transfers.
- Sponsorship, spending, paid services, financial commitments, legal formation and dissolution.

Routine work—such as bug fixes, accessibility and UX improvements, documentation updates, dependency updates, and small scoped site improvements within agreed priorities—can be delegated by mutual agreement; neither partner needs permission for every routine contribution within the agreed scope. Responsibilities follow strengths, interests and availability. Equal leadership requires neither equal working hours nor equal contribution counts or financial investment. Work asynchronously and avoid pressure to respond outside agreed availability.

## 4. Checks and balances

Both partners follow the same engineering and ethical safeguards:

- Never self-approve a pull request, bypass required checks, weaken branch protections unilaterally or retaliate for respectful disagreement.
- Either partner may merge a routine contribution only after eligible independent non-author human approval, passing required checks and resolution of required review threads.
- If both materially authored a code, security or branch-rule change, obtain qualified independent review from another human; if unavailable, defer the change rather than bypass review. For a two-partner non-code governance draft while there are only two active maintainers, explicit written mutual agreement satisfies review alongside passing required checks and an eligible non-author PR approval.
- Major decisions need mutual consent in addition to applicable engineering review. AI assistance does not replace human accountability.

A review comment, code ownership or administrative capability does not establish consent to a major decision. Aim for no more than two or three actively reviewed PRs at a time; prioritize security and one useful authorized humanitarian deliverable before starting more feature work.

## 5. Security and continuity

Use individual accounts, strong authentication, least privilege, appropriate backups, documented administrative changes and tested recovery procedures. Never share personal credentials. Before changing administrative access, agree on custody, recovery and emergency responsibilities.

Neither partner should unilaterally remove the other or change ownership or administrative permissions. A narrowly scoped emergency procedure may permit temporary containment of an imminent security threat—such as suspected account compromise or active credential or privacy exposure—with prompt notice through an agreed secondary channel when safe, a private record, independent review and restoration once secured. Agree on that procedure and review path before expanding administrative access; do not use it to resolve ordinary disagreements.

Platform owners may retain technical powers that this agreement cannot constrain. Document those limitations and verify available controls rather than promise technical protections that do not exist.

## 6. Volunteers and community

Welcome technical and nontechnical contributions, including research, accessibility, translation, design, testing and documentation. Recognize contributions fairly; protect respectful dissent, privacy and sustainable workloads. Apply the code of conduct consistently, recuse from conflicts and provide a fair route for concerns and appeals. Do not claim an independent panel exists until one is established.

Volunteer and maintainer roles are distinct from project ownership. Keep entry paths accessible, including comment-only contributions. Agree on recruitment priorities together; maintain respectful communication with existing applicants. No additional co-owners are contemplated in this proposal; any future change would require mutual agreement.

## 7. Humanitarian integrity

Listen to organizations before proposing work. Obtain authorization, scope, acceptance criteria and handoff arrangements before undertaking a deliverable; obtain separate permission to publish partner information or evidence. Protect beneficiary data. Never invent partnerships, funding, meals, beneficiaries or completed outcomes. Humanitarian assistance to beneficiaries remains free.

## 8. Financial transparency and sustainability

Sponsorships, grants, development support and disclosed paid professional services may be considered by mutual agreement. Separate development funding from food-aid donations; disclose conflicts and maintain relevant financial records accessible to both partners, subject to privacy and law.

No fundraising account, expense, financial commitment, compensation, income distribution or legal ownership arrangement is authorized by this draft. Agree on lawful arrangements before accepting obligations or distributing funds.

## 9. Disagreement, departure and accountability

Explain differences respectfully, consider evidence and alternatives, allow reasonable time for reflection and seek mutually acceptable independent mediation when needed. Keep disputed major decisions unchanged pending resolution, apart from necessary agreed emergency containment. If a major disagreement remains unresolved after a reasonable reflection window (such as seven days), either partner may propose input from a mutually trusted neutral third party before revisiting.

Both partners deserve fair notice, a chance to respond, recusal for conflicts and an impartial appeal route for serious conduct concerns. Neither should be arbitrarily displaced or stripped of historical recognition, and neither should make unilateral major changes during the other's temporary absence. Before formalizing ownership, jointly define practical procedures for voluntary departure, incapacity, serious misconduct, unresolved deadlock and succession. Do not treat these unresolved procedures as active guarantees.

## 10. Formalization and adoption

Before any organization creation, repository transfer or expansion of administrative access, both partners should agree on:

- The meaning of shared leadership, platform ownership, legal ownership and financial arrangements.
- Account security, independent review, recovery and continuity, including what GitHub can and cannot enforce.
- A practical dispute and emergency procedure, migration plan and necessary legal or financial advice.
- At least one completed, verified real-world humanitarian deliverable so organizational structure serves active work.

GitHub organization ownership is an administrative role, not proof of equal equity or legal ownership; this draft does not guarantee a technically enforced mutual veto.

Both founding participants may lead proposals, request changes and revise this text freely. Neither is expected to simply endorse the other's draft. Before adoption, reconcile the final agreement with [current governance](../../GOVERNANCE.md) and maintain live role and access facts in one dated roster ([docs/governance/ROSTER.md](ROSTER.md)) rather than duplicating them across multiple documents. Follow existing public consultation and review requirements, including the normal seven-day feedback period for substantial policy changes. Record explicit mutual agreement and applicable independent review; no automatic adoption or merge.

Revisit broader community governance (using [PR #51](https://github.com/gsel001/one-meal-forward/pull/51) as a reference) when clear growth triggers are met—such as reaching five or more active contributors, before creating a GitHub organization, or before accepting funding. After adoption, amendments require the same mutual consent and applicable review. Keep the agreement proportionate to the project's size.

**Founding principle:** Neither partner is above the mission, accountability or the other. Shared leadership means equal respect, independent judgment, mutual responsibility and a commitment to helping others.
