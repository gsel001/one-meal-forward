01 /

Choose a task.

Read the task’s requirements and comments. Choose writing, translation, research, testing, design, or development work that fits your skills.

02 /

Submit your work.

Follow the acceptance checklist and open a pull request. Link the issue, include sources or test results, and ask for help if needed.

03 /

Get a review.

An authorized reviewer checks the change before merge. Organization work also needs staff acceptance and evidence of actual use.
