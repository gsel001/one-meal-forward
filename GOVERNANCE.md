# Governance and moderation

## Stewardship

@gsel001 is the founding maintainer and initial final decision-maker. The project is an independent open-source volunteer initiative, not a registered charity. Contributions are evaluated for usefulness, evidence, accessibility, maintainability, and safety, not stars or volume. No formal partner relationships exist at launch.

Propose routine changes through issues and pull requests; use Discussions for broader ideas. Explain tradeoffs in public. Substantial policy changes should remain open for community feedback for at least seven days before a maintainer decision, unless urgent privacy or security action is needed. Maintainers document decisions and disagreements. Contributor roles may expand after sustained, careful review work; access is granted at the minimum required level and can be revoked for misuse.

## Moderation

Review new organization submissions before creating a website entry. Keep pending submissions off the public board until public evidence is safe to publish. Remove spam and scams, redact sensitive data promptly, and use GitHub's sensitive-data removal process where needed. Deleting a comment cannot recall forks, notifications, caches, or Git history. Never promise confidentiality on a public issue.

Enforce the code of conduct with proportionate clarification, warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or ban. Record a privacy-preserving reason when practical. Offer a private appeal through the maintainer's published profile contact or GitHub reporting channels. There is one maintainer initially and no independent internal appeals panel. A conflicted maintainer should recuse when another reviewer is available; GitHub Trust & Safety handles platform abuse reports.

## Mission decisions

Follow docs/verification.md. Verification is scoped to evidence and authorization for one listing; it is not blanket partner accreditation. Do not publish requests involving unsafe contact, payment to volunteer, unexplained financial transfers, beneficiary identification, or unsupported aid claims. Suspend disputed listings and ask for evidence through official organization channels. Never represent issue closure as completed field work.

## Funding

No fundraising accounts or financial commitments are authorized. Any future account, application, expense, or nonprofit claim requires founder approval. Publish a budget and distinguish development support from aid donations before accepting funds. Community participation is always free.
