# Community code of conduct

We welcome people of all backgrounds, languages, identities, abilities, and experience levels. Be kind, specific, patient, and respectful. Critique ideas and work, not people. Ask before sharing someone else's story, identity, or image. Accept constructive feedback and respect a person's decision to disengage.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized content, scams, spam, and exploitative portrayals of people experiencing hunger are unacceptable. Do not solicit sensitive beneficiary information or pressure anyone to donate, disclose personal experiences, or work unpaid beyond their chosen contribution.

This applies to issues, pull requests, Discussions, and public spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers follow the same rules.

For public spam, use GitHub's report function. For a private conduct report, contact the maintainer using the contact channel published on [@gsel001's profile](https://github.com/gsel001), if available, or GitHub's private abuse-report process. Do not post allegations or personal information publicly. There is no dedicated independent conduct team yet.

Maintainers may clarify expectations, edit or remove content, warn, temporarily restrict participation, or ban participants in proportion to severity. Serious threats or exposure of private data may require immediate restriction. Conflicts of interest require recusal where another maintainer is available. You may appeal privately with new context; if the sole maintainer is involved, GitHub Trust & Safety is the external reporting route. See [governance](GOVERNANCE.md) for process and limitations.
