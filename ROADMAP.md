# Roadmap

## Available now

Static mission board, real beginner project tasks, moderated organization intake, evidence and expiry states, an honest impact ledger, English interface, CI, browser accessibility checks, community policies, and no paid infrastructure.

## Next: community review

Complete independent accessibility and device reviews, improve beginner instructions, recruit fluent translation reviewers, and research official organization channels with public sources. See the Community launch milestone. Timeframes depend on volunteers; no partner outcomes are promised.

## First organization pilot

Seek permission before outreach on behalf of the project. Invite one willing organization to submit a small, safe request. Verify its public identity, consent, scope, safeguarding, contact channel, and expiry. Publish only after evidence review. Ask the organization for evidence of completed work before recording any contribution. See the First verified organization mission milestone.

## Later, only when justified

Release reviewed translations and RTL support; improve mission detail views and evidence review tools. Add infrastructure only when real needs justify maintenance. Avoid beneficiary data collection. Do not add donations or claim charitable status without explicit approval.
